## 1. Trump, Xi and Putin issue a "peace declaration" (SUMMIT)

After their first three-way meeting in Shenzhen on 19 November, the
leaders of the US, China and Russia release a four-point declaration:
a nuclear war "cannot be won and must never be fought", UN reform,
more refined-fuel supplies, and dialogue on reducing nuclear risks.
The text says nothing about Ukraine or Iran. The full text and the
worldwide coverage land on 20 November.

## 2. Cyclone Akvan makes landfall in Odisha (WEATHER)

Severe Cyclonic Storm Akvan crosses the coast near Gopalpur, Odisha,
around noon IST on Friday 20 November, with sustained winds of
90-100 km/h gusting to 110 km/h. More than 72,000 people evacuated;
red alert in force; heavy to extremely heavy rain.

## 3. EU-UK summit in Brussels (EUROPE)

UK Prime Minister Andy Burnham meets European Council President
Antonio Costa and European Commission President Ursula von der Leyen
in Brussels on 20 November for the second EU-UK summit: trade,
youth mobility, energy and emissions-trading deals on the table, with
a joint declaration expected. Burnham has said Brexit "has not worked".

## 4. EU summons Russian envoy after Kyiv strikes hit embassies (UKRAINE)

Russian strikes on Kyiv on 19 November damaged the Dutch and Austrian
embassies (1 killed, 5 hurt, St Volodymyr's Cathedral hit). On 20
November the EU announces it will summon Russia's ambassador; Austria
and the Netherlands summon the Russian envoy as well.
